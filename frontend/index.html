How to use Malina

To create a backup, enter the Raspberry Pi SSH address, choose an authentication method and destination folder, test the connection, then start the backup.

To restore, choose a Malina image and removable target drive, then review the erase confirmation carefully.

Use Jobs to monitor, pause, resume, or cancel operations. Jobs continue when their details are closed.

Save a device to remember its connection and backup folder. Use Devices to select, edit, or remove saved configurations. The selected device appears in the upper-right corner.
