Back up remotely. Identify the Pi's source disk over SSH, choose a local folder, and stream the complete device without writing to it.

Read the safety boundaries →
  • ✓
    Removable drives only

    Internal and system disks are excluded from destination discovery.

  • ✓
    Exact-path confirmation

    The selected target identifier is required again before erasing.

  • ✓
    Full read-back option

    Verify every written byte before automatically ejecting the drive.

  • ✓
    No partial backups

    Failed captures are cleaned up instead of looking complete.
